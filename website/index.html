Do I need an account or a connection?

No. The Windows app works locally without an account or network connection. Project files and recovery stay on your computer.

What should I save?

Save a .sunset project to keep editable marks, pasted images, and fine vector details. PNG export is a flat image of the full 4096 × 4096 canvas.

How far can I zoom? How does fill work?

The outer canvas is fixed. Zoom reaches up to 1,000,000,000,000×, with numeric precision setting the practical limit. Fill traces the visible region, so keep an enclosed boundary visible when filling it.

Can I use or change the code?

Yes. The MIT license permits use, modification, and redistribution, including commercial use. Keep the copyright and license notice with copies or substantial portions of the code.
